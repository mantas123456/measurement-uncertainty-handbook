# Contribute

!!! info "Placeholder"

Corrections and suggestions are welcome, especially from laboratory practitioners.

- **Found an error in a formula or a number?** Open an
  [issue](https://github.com/mantas123456/measurement-uncertainty-handbook/issues)
  with the page, what you expected and your source.
- **Have a suggestion for a topic or an example?** Open an issue describing the
  practical question it would answer.
- **Calculation bugs** belong in the
  [`labqc` issues](https://github.com/mantas123456/lab-quality-toolkit/issues).

Please do not send real laboratory or client data. All examples use synthetic data.
